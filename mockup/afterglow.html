On screen

Starting up

The camera boots and checks the loaded film.

    Try this

    Take a shot, then press A again before winding: the shutter stays locked, like a real camera. Drag Light down to hear and see the shake warning. Hold B for settings and load INSTANT or MONO 400. Hold A+B for Super 8.

    Sensors & controls

    Tilt stands in for the accelerometer and Light for the ambient light sensor. Sound starts after your first click.

    Use your own photo

    The browser can't use a camera here, so the lens looks at a painted lake at sunset. Photos and clips stay in this tab only.

    How it maps to the firmware

    • Each film is a 64K-entry RGB565 lookup table. The live preview costs one memory read per pixel, and the colours here are quantised to 565 exactly like the panel.
    • The darkroom scene (~2.6 s) covers the slow part: the JPEG encode and the SD card write.
    • Frame 1 of every roll gets a light leak, like film fogged while it was being loaded.
    • The meter needle and shutter speed come from the light sensor; the level line comes from the accelerometer.
    • Cine clips are stored as JPEG frames (MJPEG), the format the device will write, with sound from its mic.
    • Every control uses only A, B, hold and A+B, so it all fits the K10's two buttons.
